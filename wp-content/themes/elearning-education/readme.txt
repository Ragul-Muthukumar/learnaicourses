eLearning Education is a minimal and professional WordPress theme that has been perfectly crafted for all types of education centres.

== Description ==

eLearning Education is a minimal and professional education theme crafted for all types of academic and training institutions, making it ideal for primary and secondary schools, training centers, course hubs, universities, colleges, educational institutes, science fairs, coaching centres, vocational training, book selling, arts and crafts classes, daycare, competitive exam academies, distance learning platforms, software training institutes, and more, including use cases for e-learning and coacher-driven programs. It is user-friendly, cleanly coded, and packed with powerful features to support smooth academic operations. The design is fully translation-ready with support for RTL layouts, allowing use in multiple languages. With complete WooCommerce integration, it enables secure online payments for course enrolments, subscriptions, learning resources, books, and merchandise. Built-in event and blog sections help maintain engagement within your academic community, while a wide range of pre-built custom add-ons allows flexible customization based on specific requirements. It is fully responsive and adapts seamlessly across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices, ensuring smooth access for students, teachers, administrators, and learners. Users can stay updated, communicate effectively, access learning materials, and submit assignments with ease. Additional features include testimonial sections and advanced social media integration to connect all platforms and enhance credibility and visibility.

Installation

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in eLearning Education in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on the 'Activate' button to use your new theme right away.
4. Go to # for a guide on how to customize this theme.
